### Procedure

1.  Mark the positions where we need to place the supports.<br> 
2.  Place the HD towers in the lab. Basically, HD Towers are made by erecting props on base plates and U head at the top of props.<br>
3.  Attaching Horizontal and Diagonal Bracings to support HD Tower. This whole makes a basic frame.<br>
4.  Placing Aluminium and Timber Beam on top of the basic frame. Here, aluminum beams are placed on U heads. Timber beams are placed perpendicularly on aluminum beams.<br>
5.  Sheathing and Nailing: Plywood sheets are nailed on these H beams.<br>
6.  Placing Inner and Outer beam with BFS. Using BFS, Inner and Outer beams are made to stand.<br>
